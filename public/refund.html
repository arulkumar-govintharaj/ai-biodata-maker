Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated:

Each biodata PDF is a digital product delivered instantly, so orders cannot be cancelled after the PDF has been downloaded.

You will get a full refund if

  • Money was deducted but the PDF could not be downloaded, or

How to request a refund

Email within 7 days of payment with your Razorpay payment ID (shown on screen after payment and in your payment SMS/email). Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days to the original payment method.

Not eligible
