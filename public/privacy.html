Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This policy explains how handles your information.

1. Biodata details stay on your device

The biodata form, photo and PDF are created inside your web browser. We do not upload or store your name, phone number, address, photo, family details or horoscope details on our servers. Form details are kept temporarily in your browser’s session storage so you do not lose them while editing; they are removed when you close the browser tab or click “Clear form”.

2. AI introduction

When you click “Write with AI”, only these fields are sent to our server and to our AI provider (Anthropic) to write the paragraph: first name, gender, age, education, occupation, work location, native place, family type, siblings and the notes you typed. Phone, address, photo, parents’ names and horoscope details are never sent. We do not keep this text after the paragraph is returned.

3. Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay. Razorpay collects the payment details needed to process your payment under its own privacy policy. We receive only the payment ID and status.

4. Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Razorpay’s checkout may use cookies needed to process payments.

5. Logs

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may keep standard technical logs such as IP address and browser type for security, for a limited period.
