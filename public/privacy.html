Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This policy explains how handles your information.

1. Biodata details stay on your device

The biodata form, photo and PDF are created inside your web browser. We do not save your details, photo or PDF on our servers. Form details are kept temporarily in your browser’s session storage so you do not lose them while editing; they are removed when you close the browser tab or click “Clear form”.

2. AI features

Two optional buttons use AI. Text is sent to the AI only when you click them, is used only to produce the result, and is not stored by us:

  • Write with AI sends first name, gender, age, education, occupation, work location, native place, family type, siblings and the notes you typed.
  • Convert my answers sends your typed text answers (such as names, places, occupation, family details, expectations and address) so they can be written in Tamil or English.

Your phone number, dates of birth and photo are never sent to the AI. The AI runs on Cloudflare Workers AI (or Anthropic, if enabled), which do not use this data to train their models.

3. Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay. Razorpay collects the payment details needed to process your payment under its own privacy policy. We receive only the payment ID and status.

4. Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Razorpay’s checkout may use cookies needed to process payments.

5. Logs

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may keep standard technical logs such as IP address and browser type for security, for a limited period.
